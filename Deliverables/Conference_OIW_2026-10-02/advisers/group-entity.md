## Minimal company/group Entity proposal

These are conceptual fields and responsibilities, not existing wire keypaths or canonical purpose references.

1. **Separate principals.** A company/group Entity owns its organizational artifacts and governed signing authority. Each employee retains their own Entity and domain-scoped Identity. A membership edge links a company-context identity to that company, with issuer, subject, role, exact capabilities, validity, status, authority proof and version. A person may belong to multiple companies; leaving one does not transfer or erase the person's Entity.
2. **Separate display/legal claim/verification.** Start with an organization profile and self-declared name. Optional legal-entity reference is a claim with issuer/source, jurisdiction if supplied, time and verification status. Optional registry validation and representative proof are separate. A registered company number does not prove that this requester may act for it; an unverified group should still be able to collaborate while visibly labeled. This is an architecture distinction, not a legal assessment.
3. **Give roles scoped grants.** Proposed roles: member (read agreed workspace), contributor (submit drafts), publisher (publish selected corporate artifacts), administrator (invite/revoke scoped memberships), steward/auditor (review evidence/corrections). A role label alone grants nothing. Use explicit requester-bound contracts, keypath/actions, purpose, audience, expiry and retention. Company access never sweeps in an employee's personal Entity.
4. **Support multiple administrators without sharing a person's signing key.** Bind each admin separately. Record delegation chain and administration events. Membership administrators cannot silently appoint a new ultimate owner. Use a stated recovery/quorum rule for changing control or removing the final administrator; ordinary artifact edits can remain single-author. Joint control is a missing governance contract, not something GeneralCell membership already proves.
5. **Revocation is an event, with a defined effect boundary.** Reject future reads/writes/feeds after revocation and expiry; revalidate at final side effect after asynchronous work. Keep signed revocation/version and stale-cache state visible. Do not claim previously authorized exports can be remotely erased. Employment termination, conference access, organization representation and storage permission are separately revocable.
6. **Share artifacts rather than ingesting people.** A shared artifact has stable ID/version, author acting personally or under a named organization delegation, original source, capture/transformation history, claim/support/counterclaim references, allowed audience, purpose, storage/disclosure grant and correction status. Preserve dissent as separate attributed assertions. A correction supersedes a claim/projection; it does not silently rewrite who asserted what. Avoid a single numerical public score for a person or company.
7. **Use portable owner storage and derived views.** Each personal/company/conference owner has a canonical authorized home and exportable artifacts/receipts. Conference search/graph is a rebuildable projection over explicitly contributed artifacts. Read, write, execute and storage authority are separate; storage does not imply disclosure. A reference may resolve remotely when granted, but a UUID or endpoint itself conveys no authority. Offline edits show pending/conflict status until acknowledged; no success toast from a local queue alone.
8. **Choose conservative consistency first.** Immutable versions for published artifacts/evidence/grants; owner-coordinated membership/admin changes; explicit multi-value conflict for concurrent drafts; rebuildable search/graph indexes. Do not start by promising unrestricted multi-master writes to personal truth. Existing canonical-home enrollment is a smaller integration seam than a new universal distributed database.

## Hardest premise and strongest counterproposal

**Root claim C1:** “Making shared conference knowledge the primary purpose will increase collective value.” Status: open, owner Kjetil/product; plausible normative/product hypothesis, not established by source existence.

- Support: sourced artifacts, resolved questions and reusable explanations can help people beyond the original meeting. The code already supplies useful provenance/private-projection and claim-model pieces.
- Strong undercut: more ingestion may increase quantity while decreasing correctness, consent, trust and willingness to participate. Employment ties and inferred interests can leak through relationship cardinality and discovery even if raw conversations stay private. “Everyone benefits” cannot justify publishing on someone's behalf.
- Falsifier: participants can achieve the same or better observable knowledge outcome through a small curated, opt-in corpus while disclosing less personal data and incurring less correction burden. No real comparison was run here; do not attach a probability or ROI.
- Fair steelman: collective usefulness can be the product's priority without a disclosure obligation. Contribution may be pseudonymous, artifact-only, company-authorized, delayed, or absent. Private personal benefit remains available to non-contributors.

**Strongest counterproposal:** launch a curated conference knowledge commons first: participant-approved takeaways, organizer-provided sources, questions, answers and an explicit correction/dissent queue. Give every participant a private personal notebook/Entity export. Corporate participation initially publishes authorized artifacts with verified delegation only where required; defer full multi-admin company governance and general-purpose cross-scaffold replica sync. This is less ambitious but directly tests the primary value claim and uses narrower authority boundaries.

Proposed pilot gates, thresholds to be agreed rather than represented as achieved:

| Purpose / test | Observable metric | Baseline | Proposed acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shared knowledge is useful | Blind source-grounded answer/task evaluation against event-only corpus; participant opt-in usefulness feedback | Measure before pilot | Agreed improvement over curated baseline, with no higher unsupported-claim rate; no individual ranking |
| Contributions are accountable | Accepted artifacts with source/provenance, audience, grant and correction route | Unknown | 100% of accepted artifacts; reject incomplete publication |
| Correction and dissent work | Seed an erroneous claim and a substantive counterclaim; measure correction propagation and preserved authorship | Not tested | Both remain discoverable with current status; all authorized projections update within declared SLA |
| Personal independence | Non-contributor completes core conference and private enrichment journeys; company departure leaves personal data/control intact | Not tested | All named journeys pass without coerced publication or employer access |
| Cross-scaffold behavior is real | Two separately running scaffolds plus restart/offline/replay/wrong-domain/revoked-member cases | Not demonstrated | Signed request → authorized commit receipt → read-back from second scaffold; all denial cases reject and no false success |
| Governance is safe enough for company use | Two independent admins, scoped publisher, revoked member, recovery, last-admin guard, in-flight revoke | No company test found | All positive and negative stories pass with current source SHA/config evidence |

These are candidate goals under the existing `purpose://prompt.unknown` intake until canonical purpose refs are resolved; no invented purpose URI is asserted as existing. Human choice between the full design and counterproposal remains open; evidence audit is complete within this scope.
