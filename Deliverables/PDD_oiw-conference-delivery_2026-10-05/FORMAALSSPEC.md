# OIW purpose specification

Authority: Kjetil's explicit 2026-10-05 approval recorded in STATUS.md. Canonical detail: ../Conference_OIW_2026-10-02/PLAN.md.

Primary purpose: enrich the shared understanding constituted by a conference through voluntary, attributable, correctable contributions. Secondary purpose: participants enrich their own Entities under their own authority.

| Leaf purpose | Work | Observable goal |
|---|---|---|
| Independent participation and discoverability | WP1 | Two independent publishers join an umbrella without receiving authority over each other. |
| Shared understanding with provenance | WP2 | Approved version/source/audience is readable; private drafts and notes are absent from other requesters' state. |
| Useful encounters | WP3 | Live offers/needs/time produce explained matches; double booking and unauthorized confirmation fail. |
| Deliberate assistance | WP4 | Purpose/argument proposals disclose source and uncertainty and require participant acceptance. |
| Portable personal enrichment | WP5 | Real second runtime retains exact approved contribution after restart and reconciles lost response. |
| Scoped company representation | WP6 | Multiple authorized representatives; revocation/expiry deny subsequent company action without touching private Entity. |
| Reachable equivalent surfaces | WP7 | Web/native actual host actions and content match with no fake scanner controls. |
| Mutual meeting records | WP8 | Two current approvals; changed plan/content invalidates approval; revoke prevents capture/sharing. |
| Reproducible semantic simulation | WP9 | Swarm executes domain journey, records expected/actual and proves fixture cleanup. |
| Controlled learning with guests | WP10 | Pilot outcomes come from consenting invited participants; failure and rollback path usable. |

Candidate purpose refs are confined to this deliverable; no Book23 catalog mutation. Existing refs: purpose://event.participation, purpose://event.privacy-preserving-networking, purpose://contact.communication, purpose://governance, purpose://access.audit.privacy.
